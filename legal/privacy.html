Crapple Maps

Privacy Policy

Crapple Maps helps you find public restrooms nearby and keep a private log of your own visits. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you have over it.

Effective July 27, 2026 Last updated July 27, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your account, your location while you use the map, and the content you choose to add.
  • Your personal visit logs are private by default — visible only to you unless you share them with people you approve.
  • Restrooms, reviews, and codes you contribute are public, so others can find them.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we don't show third-party ads.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, right from the app.

We ask for location permission only when a feature needs it, and you can revoke it anytime in your device settings — the app still works, it just can't auto-center on you.

How we use your information

  • Show you nearby restrooms and let you save, review, and navigate to them.
  • Keep your personal log of visits and any photos you add.
  • Operate the follow system so you can share your logs with people you approve.
  • Moderate contributed content and prevent spam and abuse.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the app.

What's public and what's private

Your visit logs are private by default. They're visible only to you, unless you approve a follower to see them. Restrooms, reviews, ratings, and access codes you contribute are shared publicly so the community can benefit from them.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: remove your account, logs, and photos at any time from within the app.
  • Access & correct: view and edit the content you've added.
  • Location: turn location access on or off in your device settings.
  • Export or request removal: contact us and, where the law provides it (such as the GDPR or CCPA), we'll honor requests to access, export, or delete your data.

Data retention & security

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal logs and photos and disassociate you from any public contributions. We use encryption in transit and access controls to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is ever completely secure.

Children

Crapple Maps is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where required), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll remove it.

International users

Your information may be processed on servers located outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We'll revise the "last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide a more prominent notice in the app.
