Delete Your Account

Last updated August 5, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Crapple Maps account (developer: Austin Taylor) and exactly what happens to your data when you do.

Delete it yourself, in the app

Deletion is immediate and does not require contacting us:

  1. Open Crapple Maps and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Your account and the data listed below are removed right away. This cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials (email, Apple, or Google).
  • Your profile — username, display name, and avatar.
  • Your logs, including notes, ratings, and timestamps.
  • Photos you uploaded.
  • Your reviews and comments.
  • Your saved restrooms, follows, and follow requests.

What is kept

  • Restrooms you added to the map stay on the map, disconnected from you and no longer attributed to your account. They are shared community map data that other people rely on, so removing them would degrade the map for everyone.
  • Restroom entry codes and edits you contributed remain, anonymized, for the same reason.

Nothing retained after deletion identifies you.

Retention

Deleted account data is removed from the live database immediately. Encrypted infrastructure backups may retain copies for up to 30 days before rolling off, after which no copy remains.

Can't get into the app?

If you can't sign in, email help@crapplemaps.com from the address on the account and ask for deletion. We'll verify ownership and delete it for you, normally within 7 days.
